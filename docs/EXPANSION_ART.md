# Artes da expansão

Geradas com a ferramenta integrada de imagem, usando os sprites de massinha existentes como referências. Exportadas com alfa preservado para `public/assets/v4`. O empacotador recorta margens, separa as duas poses do gavião e converte para WebP; ele não desenha os animais.

## Prompts utilizados

### snake.webp
A single tall straight brown clay tree trunk with a small green arboreal snake coiled twice around its UPPER end, head extending slightly to the right just below the cut face. Entire trunk and snake fully visible. Straight wood core, cut top at y near 10%, stump extends to bottom. Snake clearly readable, friendly natural neutral face, not villain. Vertical 1:3 subject with generous transparent margins. Match reference sculpted handcrafted clay. No background, floor, text, glow. Game obstacle sprite.

### hawk-0.webp / hawk-1.webp
Two animation poses of the SAME brown and cream hawk flying LEFT, side view, in a horizontal 2-cell sprite sheet with wide padding between poses. Left cell wings up, right cell wings extended horizontally. Identical body, head position and size in both. Rounded handmade clay like reference bird, natural predatory bird hooked small beak, brown wings, cream chest, warm ochre feet, alert expression NOT angry cartoon villain. Whole silhouette fits each cell, isolated on transparent background. No text, no floor, no scenery.

### hollow.webp
A single very tall narrow hollow old tree trunk for a side scrolling game, sculpted brown clay matching reference. Open hollow cut top with irregular broken wood rim, moss and small side broken branch halfway down, rounded safe stylization. Straight vertical wood core, full silhouette, long shaft, generous transparent padding. No snake. No floor, no background, no text. Sprite isolated alpha.

### perch.webp
Same exact red-browed green clay parrot identity as reference, perched on a short horizontal mossy tree branch, body 3/4 facing right, wings folded, orange feet gripping branch, red forehead blue eye ring, cheerful attentive expression. Full body with tail and full short branch. Hand sculpted clay, warm soft lighting. Isolated transparent alpha background, no text, no floor, no scenery.

### feather.webp
One magical golden feather collectible sprite, sculpted soft handmade clay, curved plump feather with detailed rounded barbs, warm gold shaft, two tiny four-point gold sparkles beside feather. Simple readable silhouette with generous padding, soft warm lighting, no external bloom, no background, no text, transparent alpha. Same whimsical clay game style.

### shield.webp
Single collectible icon for a handmade clay forest game: a translucent emerald green protective bubble enclosing three plump sculpted green leaves, luminous pale mint rim, soft warm highlight, chunky rounded toy clay style. Entire circular bubble isolated, centered, generous transparent alpha margins. No text, no letters, no background, no floor, no giant glow outside silhouette.

### branch.webp
Horizontal tree branch game obstacle, handmade clay forest style. One thick short brown branch grows from RIGHT toward LEFT, broken blunt tip at left; a green small arboreal snake coiled around branch, head looking LEFT at its left tip, neutral natural friendly eyes, no angry villain. Small moss patches. Entire object fully visible, centered, 3:1 horizontal silhouette with generous transparent alpha margin. Warm sculpted clay texture, vibrant greens, rounded shapes. No vertical trunk, no background, no floor, no text.
